The SVG files display official artwork inside circular frames. Ant Group and GigaBrain use SVG viewports to show only their graphical marks, with organization names kept as text in the profile. Their embedded originals, and those for BUAA and Cybopal, remain byte-identical to the downloads.

The Noetix icon extracts the colored symbol from the official header logo: achromatic wordmark/background pixels are made transparent, the symbol is cropped to its bounds and resized to fit the circular frame without recoloring. The complete original PNG remains unchanged in `originals/noetix.png`.

Images are embedded so each profile SVG is self-contained on GitHub. Original PNG/JPEG formats are retained rather than changing extensions or converting source artwork.
